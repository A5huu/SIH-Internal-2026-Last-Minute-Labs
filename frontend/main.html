How the Risk Score is Calculated

FreightIQ quantifies chartering risk by fusing statistical rate anomalies, port turnaround constraints, meteorological models, and geopolitical telemetry into a Composite Risk Index (CRI).

Multivariate Fusion Model
Composite Risk Formula Score Range: 0 (Ultra-Safe) to 100 (Critical Disruption)
Composite Risk Score = (0.35 × Volatility) + (0.25 × Geopolitical) + (0.20 × Port Congestion) + (0.20 × Weather)
Freight Volatility (35%)
Geopolitical & Fuel (25%)
Port Congestion (20%)
Weather Risk (20%)

1. Freight Rate Volatility

35% Weight
Core Metrics: 7-Day Rate Momentum (Δ7d) & 30-Day Rolling Volatility (CV = σ / μ)
Δ7d = (R_current - R_7d_ago) / R_7d_ago
Detection Thresholds:
  • High (>75 pts): 7-day rate surge > 12% or CV > 15% (90th percentile historical spike).
  • Medium (40–74 pts): Moderate upward momentum (Δ7d between +6% and +12%).
  • Low (<40 pts): Fluctuations contained within normal trading bands.
Sources: Baltic Exchange (BDI/BSI/BPI) & Daily Spot Corridor Fixtures

2. Geopolitical & Fuel Shocks

25% Weight
Core Metrics: VLSFO/MGO Bunker Price Spikes, Trade Sanctions & Chokepoint Deviations
S_geo = max(Bunker_Shock, Sanction_Verification, Route_Deviation_Days)
Detection Thresholds:
  • High (>75 pts): Active trade restrictions, Red Sea/Suez diversions (+14 days via Cape), or bunker price jumps > $25/MT.
  • Medium (40–74 pts): Export tariff adjustments or compliance tightening on origin coal flows.
  • Low (<40 pts): Standard open navigation and stable marine fuel benchmarks.
Sources: Lloyd's List Maritime Bulletins & Singapore/Fujairah 0.5% VLSFO

3. Port Congestion & Draft

20% Weight
Core Metrics: Pre-Berthing Anchorage Wait Time (T_wait) & Under-Keel Clearance (UKC)
UKC = Berth_Draft_Envelope - Vessel_Arrival_Draft
Detection Thresholds:
  • High (>75 pts): T_wait > 48 hours, mechanical conveyor breakdowns, or UKC margin < 0.5m.
  • Medium (40–74 pts): Queue buildup (24–48h wait) or compulsory lightering required at anchorage.
  • Low (<40 pts): Direct berthing available within 24h of EOSP (End of Sea Passage).
Sources: Port Authority Circulars (Paradip, Haldia, Vizag) & Live AIS Terminal Feeds

4. Weather & Cyclonic Risk

20% Weight
Core Metrics: Bay of Bengal Depression Alerts, Significant Wave Height (Hs) & Wind Force
S_wx = f(Cyclone_Category, Hs > 2.5m, Beaufort_Force > 7)
Detection Thresholds:
  • High (>75 pts): IMD Cyclone Alert, port operations suspended, or Hs > 2.5m stopping discharge.
  • Medium (40–74 pts): Seasonal monsoon depressions (SW/NE) causing minor anchorage swells.
  • Low (<40 pts): Calm sea state (Beaufort scale 1–3), no meteorological advisories.
Sources: India Meteorological Department (IMD) & Global Marine Routing Forecasts
